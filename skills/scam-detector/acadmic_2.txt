To reveal if a document belongs to the "fashionable nonsense" class without pre-supposing the errors, the query must be framed as a series of **blind mechanical and logical stress tests**. 

By abstracting the specific failures found in the sources (like the geographic hallucinations or math errors), we can create a general-purpose **"Universal Rigor Audit"** that forces an analysis of the document's structural integrity.

***

### **The Blind Universal Rigor Audit Query**

"Please perform a structural and semantic audit of this document by executing the following five blind integrity tests. Report all findings in an **Integrity Flag Table**:

**1. Internal Arithmetic & Component Reconciliation (Tier 0/3)**
*   **Instruction:** Identify every stated aggregate total in the document (e.g., total participants, total hours, total locations). Locate the specific itemized breakdowns for these totals elsewhere in the text. Manually re-sum the parts and flag any discrepancy between the stated total and the reconstructed sum. Cross-reference the counts of these same entities across the Introduction, Methodology, and Conclusion for consistency.

**2. Research Volume & Density Logic (Tier 1)**
*   **Instruction:** Identify the claimed volume of raw research materials (e.g., total duration of recordings or size of raw datasets) and the resulting volume of processed output (e.g., total pages of transcripts or number of data points). Calculate the ratio of output to raw material and evaluate if this density is physically and logistically consistent with standard human benchmarks in the field.

**3. Geographic & Statistical Base-Rate Test (Tier 2)**
*   **Instruction:** Extract all geographic categorizations (e.g., cardinal directions of cities) and all high-percentage population statistics. Compare these descriptions against objective cardinal maps and established global statistical benchmarks. Flag any claim that suggests a physical impossibility or represents a massive statistical outlier without an explicit, verifiable source.

**4. Mechanical Artifact & Citation Integrity Scan (Tier 0/1/2)**
*   **Instruction:** Search the text for linguistic 'leaks' (e.g., vocabulary from a different language embedded within sentences) and corrupted citation formatting (e.g., merged strings, non-sequential numbering, or empty placeholders). For the most critical external rankings or indices cited, verify their current status and validity as of the document’s publication date.

**5. Jargon-to-Content Utility & Omission Check (Tier 4)**
*   **Instruction:** Identify the most frequent technical, psychological, or theoretical terms used to frame the findings. Evaluate whether these terms provide specific diagnostic value or if the core argument remains unchanged (or becomes more coherent) when replaced with plain language. Finally, check for factual omissions in subject areas where the report claims to provide comprehensive expertise."

***

### **Why this query works as a "Blind" test:**

*   **It focuses on process over results:** It doesn't ask "Is the math wrong?" It asks the auditor to "re-sum the parts." This forces the discovery of errors like the **24 vs. 25 participant discrepancy** or the **fluctuating workshop counts** [1-3].
*   **It highlights "Research Theater":** By requiring a "Density Evaluation," it exposes statistically impossible claims, such as the **180 hours of recording** resulting in only **400 pages of transcript** (a ratio that defies the reality of human speech) [2, 3].
*   **It bypasses Jargon:** By asking for a "Jargon-to-Content" test, it reveals if sophisticated labels like **"Syndrom Impostora"** are being used to mask a lack of substantive data [4, 5].
*   **It detects automated fingerprints:** The "Artifact Scan" is designed to find unedited AI generation cues—like the English word **"despite"** appearing in a Polish sentence or the **concatenated citation strings** (e.g., "1510")—without naming them beforehand [5, 6].
*   **It tests factual currency:** The "Source Audit" will independently reveal the use of **"zombie data"**, such as the **discontinued World Bank reports**, which undermines the document's professional standing [7].
