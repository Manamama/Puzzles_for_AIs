A note on "middle" and on "two", as concepts. 

"Middle" is polysemous: between two things is one; inside one of them is another; the halfway point of something is a third; the middle of a group is a fourth. Which sense is in play changes which thing is "in the middle" — and so which colour may be the answer. 

"Two" does not mean "only two", either. Think outside the box, starting with what words may mean indeed. 

So: things may not be as you are used to. Solve now.
